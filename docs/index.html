About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Evomics Lab at Sun Yat-sen University Cancer Center. My research focuses on telomere variation and viral pangenomics.

Research Interests

  • Telomeres: length variation, variant repeats, and DNA methylation.
  • Viral pangenomics: papillomavirus genome diversity and evolution.
  • Bioinformatics: methods and software for long-read sequencing.
